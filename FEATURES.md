# STAR WARS Zero Company — Features

STAR WARS Zero Company squad tools for PC with progression controls, campaign resources, equipment profiles and mission practice. Build your squad around the way you play and compare tactical setups across repeatable encounters.

## Squad progression

Adjust squad development and individual character values.

## Mission practice

Save campaign snapshots and replay missions with a different tactical setup.

## Campaign resources

Set resource values and prepare an equipment budget for the squad.

## Build profiles

Save alternative combinations of squad roles, equipment and progression settings.

## Combat assistance

Adjust incoming damage and ability-use settings for combat practice.

## Campaign history

Organize saved profiles by mission and return to the campaign state you need.

## Profiles

Save a campaign profile before a mission, prepare two squad builds and compare their performance. Keep the configuration that suits your tactics.
